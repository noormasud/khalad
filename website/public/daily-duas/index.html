Duas

Daily duas from the Sunnah

Duas for sleeping, waking up, leaving the house and using the toilet, with meaning and references.

Short supplications from the Sunnah for the everyday moments of your day, each with its reference.

Before sleeping

Bismika Allahumma amutu wa ahya.

With Your name, O Allah, I die and I live.

Sahih al-Bukhari 6324 (Hudhaifah)

When waking up

Al-hamdu lillahil-ladhi ahyana ba'da ma amatana wa ilaihin-nushur.

All praise is for Allah, Who made us alive after He made us die (sleep), and unto Him is the Resurrection.

Sahih al-Bukhari 6324 (Hudhaifah)

When leaving the house

Bismillah, tawakkaltu 'alallah, la hawla wa la quwwata illa billah.

In the name of Allah, I trust in Allah; there is no might and no power but in Allah.

Whoever says it is told: “You are guided, defended and protected.”

Sunan Abi Dawud 5095 (Anas ibn Malik), graded sahih by al-Albani

Before entering the toilet

Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal-khubuthi wal-khaba'ith.

O Allah, I seek refuge with You from all offensive and wicked things.

Sahih al-Bukhari 142 (Anas ibn Malik)

After leaving the toilet

Ghufranaka.

(I ask) Your forgiveness.

Sunan Abi Dawud 30 ('A'isha), graded sahih by al-Albani

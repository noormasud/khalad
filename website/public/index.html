Built for Muslims in Pakistan and around the world, in English and Urdu.

Prayer times, quietly

Accurate times for where you are, Hanafi or standard Asr. The next prayer sits on your lock screen. No adhan going off at the wrong moment.

A daily Quran goal

From one ayah to one juz a day, with Urdu and English translation. Pick up exactly where you left off.

Adhkar that open and count

Morning, evening, night and after salah. Tap to count, pinch to resize, meaning one tap away.

What to look for in an Islamic app

Whichever app you choose, these are the things that matter most for daily worship.

Prayer times that match your masjid

Calculated for your exact location, with the usual method for your region and a choice of Hanafi or standard Asr. You should be able to adjust each prayer by a few minutes.

A Quran text you can trust

The Arabic should come from a known source, such as Quran.com, with translations named so you know whose words you are reading.

Hadith with references

Every hadith should show where it comes from, like Sahih al-Bukhari 6465, so you can check it yourself.

Nothing that distracts from worship

No ads in the middle of adhkar, no popups, no noise you didn't ask for.

Respect for your privacy

You shouldn't need an account to read the Quran, and your location shouldn't leave your phone.

Help with consistency

Small daily goals, gentle reminders for Surah Al-Mulk and Al-Kahf, and a streak that forgives a missed day.

Fasting on Mondays and Thursdays

Why the Prophet ﷺ fasted on Mondays and Thursdays, with the authentic hadith and how to keep the fast.

Fasting on Mondays and Thursdays is a voluntary Sunnah fast that many Muslims keep through the year.

Why Monday and Thursday

How to keep it

  • Make your intention the night before, and eat Suhoor before Fajr.
  • Break your fast at Maghrib.
  • Skip it on the days of Eid and the days of Tashriq. In Ramadan you are already fasting.

Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

The short version

  • No ads, no advertising ID, and we never sell data.
  • No account or sign-up. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your location, reading progress and sadaqah stay on your phone.
  • Anonymous usage statistics help us improve the app. They're on by default and you can turn them off any time.

Who we are

Khalad is published by Khalad LTD (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about your privacy, email khalad.global@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Most of what Khalad knows about you never leaves your device:

  • Location. If you allow it, Khalad uses your approximate location, rounded to about 1 km, to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is used on your phone only and is never sent to us. You can choose a city instead.
  • Your progress. Reading goal, pages and ayahs read, streak, adhkar and surahs completed, tasbih counts.
  • Sadaqah. Your monthly goal and the amounts you log. These are never sent anywhere.
  • Settings. Calculation method, madhab, reminders, language, text size and theme.

We don't back this up to our servers. Uninstalling Khalad, or clearing its storage, deletes it.

What is sent to our servers

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which features are used and what to improve, Khalad sends small usage events. This is on by default. Turn it off any time in More → Share anonymous usage, or during setup.

Each event contains: a random ID created on your phone (not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account), the event name (for example “screen opened”, “adhkar completed”, “setting changed”), a few simple values (for example the reading goal you picked, the text size, which reminder you turned on, or counts rounded into ranges), the app version and the time.

Events never contain your location, your name or contact details, your sadaqah amounts or anything you type.

We keep individual events for 90 days. After that we keep only daily totals, which contain no ID at all.

Ummah goals

When you add your recitation to a shared Ummah goal, the app creates an anonymous account (no email or name) so the count can be added safely and abuse can be limited. We store the count and that anonymous account ID.

Downloading content

The app downloads hadith, adhkar, reminders and app settings from our database. As with any internet request, the servers that answer see your device's IP address in standard logs.

Services we use

  • Supabase hosts our database: content, anonymous usage events and Ummah goal counts.
  • Quran Foundation (Quran.com). The Quran text and translations are downloaded from their service, which sees your IP address when you download a surah.
  • Google Play delivers the app and its updates under Google's own privacy policy. If we offer an optional supporter subscription in future, payment will be handled by Google Play; we never see your card details.

Khalad contains no advertising or tracking SDKs, and it does not use the Android advertising ID. Reminders and prayer times are scheduled on your phone; we don't run push-notification servers.

Children

Khalad is suitable for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included: the app asks for no name, email or contact details.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off usage statistics in More → Share anonymous usage.
  • Refuse location access and choose your city instead.
  • Turn off notifications in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Delete everything on your phone by uninstalling Khalad or clearing its storage.

Because usage events use a random ID that isn't linked to you, we can't normally tell which events are yours. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Email khalad.global@gmail.com and we'll help however we can.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Our database only allows the app to read published content and to add anonymous events; the app cannot read anyone's usage data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects what is collected, we'll tell you in the app before it applies.

Qibla direction in Leeds

The Qibla from Leeds is 119° from north (ESE). Here is how to face it.

Face 119° from north (ESE), which is 29° south of due east.

Qibla angle
119.5° clockwise from true north
Direction
ESE, 29° south of due east
Distance to the Kaaba
5,002 km (3,108 miles)

How to face the Qibla in Leeds

  1. Open your phone's compass app and hold the phone flat.
  2. Turn slowly until the compass shows 119°.
  3. The top of your phone now points to the Qibla.

This is calculated for the centre of Leeds. Anywhere in the city the difference is a fraction of a degree.

Qibla direction in Manchester

The Qibla from Manchester is 118° from north (ESE). Here is how to face it.

Face 118° from north (ESE), which is 28° south of due east.

Qibla angle
118.5° clockwise from true north
Direction
ESE, 28° south of due east
Distance to the Kaaba
5,025 km (3,122 miles)

How to face the Qibla in Manchester

  1. Open your phone's compass app and hold the phone flat.
  2. Turn slowly until the compass shows 118°.
  3. The top of your phone now points to the Qibla.

This is calculated for the centre of Manchester. Anywhere in the city the difference is a fraction of a degree.

Questions

When should I read Surah Al-Kahf?

On Friday. Many Muslims read it in the morning or before Jummah.

How long is Surah Al-Kahf?

Surah Al-Kahf is the 18th surah, with 110 ayat. Most people read it in about 30 to 45 minutes, and some split it across the day.

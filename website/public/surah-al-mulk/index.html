Surah Al-Mulk at night

The virtue of Surah Al-Mulk and the Sunnah of reading it every night before sleeping, with authentic references.

Surah
67, Al-Mulk (The Dominion), also called Tabarak
Ayat
30
When
At night, before sleeping

The virtue of Surah Al-Mulk

The Sunnah of reading it at night

So the Sunnah is to read it every night, along with Surah As-Sajdah (32) if you can.

Questions

When should I read Surah Al-Mulk?

Many Muslims read it every night before sleeping, following the Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ.

How long is Surah Al-Mulk?

Surah Al-Mulk is the 67th surah of the Quran, with 30 ayat. It takes most people about 5 to 10 minutes to read.

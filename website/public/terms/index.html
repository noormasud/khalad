Prayer times, Qibla and dates

  • Prayer times are calculated from your location using established methods (for example University of Islamic Sciences Karachi, ISNA or Muslim World League) and your chosen Asr method. They can differ by a few minutes from your local masjid's timetable, especially for Fajr, Isha and at high latitudes. You can adjust each prayer in settings.
  • For fasting, Suhoor and Iftar, follow your local masjid or trusted authority.
  • The Qibla direction is calculated and depends on your phone's compass, which can be affected by magnets and metal. Please confirm it where possible.
  • Hijri dates are calculated and can differ by a day from local moon sighting. You can adjust them in settings.
